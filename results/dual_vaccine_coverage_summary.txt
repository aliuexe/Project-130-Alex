4. WHY CLASS-AWARE TRACKING MATTERS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Immunological Synergy: CD4+ T-cell help (via MHC Class II 15-mers) is required
   to prime, sustain, and prevent exhaustion of CD8+ cytotoxic T-cells (via MHC Class I 9-mers).
b) Class-Aware vs Naive Tracking: The naive 'ge2' metric counts tumours hit by >= 2 distinct
   mutations, but does not distinguish whether both hits are Class I (no CD4+ help) or one
   is Class I and one is Class II (genuine CD4+/CD8+ synergy). The 'Synergy' metric
   specifically counts tumours with >= 1 Class I AND >= 1 Class II epitope.
c) Stated Limitation: The MHC-II binding predictions use a simplified 4-anchor-position
   PSSM (P1, P4, P6, P9). Non-anchor positions (P2, P3, P5, P7, P8) are not scored.
   A full SMM-align or NetMHCIIpan predictor would provide more accurate binding estimates.
