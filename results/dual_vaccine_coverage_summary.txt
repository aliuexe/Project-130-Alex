3. WHY THE DUAL COCKTAIL DRASTICALLY IMPROVES BIOLOGICAL PLAUSIBILITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
a) Immunological Synergy: CD4+ T-cell help (via MHC Class II 15-mers) is required
   to prime, sustain, and prevent exhaustion of CD8+ cytotoxic T-cells (via MHC Class I 9-mers).
b) Overcoming Mutual Exclusivity: In Class I alone, KRAS G12D, G12V, G13D are mutually
   exclusive across patients, so a patient with KRAS G12D often only receives 1 Class I epitope.
   Adding Class II 15-mers targeting DRB1*15:01 / *07:01 enables simultaneous CD4+ helper
   and CD8+ cytotoxic targeting, causing multi-epitope (>=2) coverage to surge!
