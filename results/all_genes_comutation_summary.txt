2. KEY FINDINGS & LESSONS FROM GENOME-WIDE TESTING
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Giant Gene Bias: Raw patient overlap counts are heavily dominated by giant structural/extracellular genes
  such as TTN, MUC16, SYNE1, OBSCN, RYR2, USH2A. These genes accumulate dozens of passenger mutations purely due to
  their massive coding length (e.g. TTN is ~33,000 amino acids).
- Confounding by Gene Popularity: Highly mutated gene pairs (e.g. TTN + MUC16) have large raw overlaps
  simply because both genes are mutated in many tumours, NOT because they share a functional pathway.
- Multiple-Testing Penalty: Testing all 154.6 million pairs imposes an extreme FDR penalty of m = 154,607,320,
  requiring an unadjusted p-value < 3.2e-10 to achieve FDR q < 0.05.
